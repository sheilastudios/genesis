# Evidence status

This is an original, high-level synthesis of two retained research proposals, not a release of those papers or a report of a new experiment.

## What was inspected

The preparation review read the complete papers titled *The Genesis Seed: Endogenous Architecture Growth in Continuous Artificial Agency* and *The Genesis Seed v3: Hard Fitness Mechanics and Endogenous Architecture Growth*. Exact source identities and inspection records are retained privately.

The selected v3 laptop and VPS copies matched byte for byte. The earlier paper is a different artifact. A matching copy is not an independent study, and a filename marked final is not evidence of peer review, accepted publication or an executed evaluation.

Neither inspected paper supplies an exact Genesis trial-result file, run receipt or measured-results table. This is a gap in these sources, not a claim that no testing exists anywhere.

## Their contributions are proposals

| Material | What it contributes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Earlier proposal | Agent-created continuity practices; comparisons with generic guidance and fixed externally supplied scaffolding; a distinction between provenance and stronger ownership behavior | Executed comparisons, net benefit, or an independent-authorship result |
| v3 proposal | Outcome-sensitive feedback, adaptation versus mere consequences, and attention to inaction, concealment, evaluator gaming and repair overhead | A demonstrated effect, a necessary punitive mechanism, or reliable evaluator behavior |
| Both papers | Behavior rather than architectural prose as the relevant target; reuse, usefulness, alternative explanations and ways the hypothesis could fail | Completed measurements or convergence across independent systems |

The earlier paper explicitly includes a fixed-scaffolding comparison. V3 adds a consequence-only comparison without explicitly retaining that earlier comparison in its listed conditions. This edition discusses both considerations; it does not present them as one frozen or executed experimental design.

## What is inherited and what is editorial

The research question, concern about supplied infrastructure, distinction between documents and behavior, proposed comparisons, maintenance costs and failure possibilities come from the retained proposals.

The short help-page example, reader-facing organization and explanation of evidential limits are newly authored editorial synthesis. They are not recovered experimental observations or additions already adopted into a running system.

## Stronger claims need stronger evidence

Evidence for later use would need to connect an identified practice to subsequent behavior. Evidence for improvement would additionally need credible comparisons and accounting for useful progress and maintenance costs. Reduced failure through avoiding work is not enough, and this edition supplies no universal score or measured threshold.

An encoding-specific benefit would need evidence separating representation from changes in guidance, resources, feedback or scaffolding. Neither inspected paper provides such an executed comparison. This statement does not adjudicate separate research on compact representations.

Unfamiliar environments do not erase prior learned skills. Repeated vocabulary does not establish convergence on useful functions. System-level changes in files and strategy are not, by themselves, model-weight learning, independent authorship or subjective-experience evidence.

Results from related projects are not imported as Genesis evidence. No current companion, memory or continuing-session architecture is selected by this retrospective, and no source protocol is activated.

## Scope of this edition

No experiment, provider call, software execution, runtime change or reproduction of historical trials was performed for this publication. The edition contains no governing prompt, detailed enforcement or repair recipe, compressed source block, private transcript or manuscript.

Missing results prevent a demonstrated-benefit claim. They do not prevent a clearly labelled, falsifiable research question from being useful.
