# Contributing to Genesis

Genesis invites scrutiny, not demonstrations of a predetermined conclusion. You do not need to agree with the hypothesis to contribute.

This repository currently presents a research hypothesis and evidence framework, plus a public [discussion seed](DISCUSSION-SEED.md), not a frozen executable protocol. Helping sharpen the question is useful participation now. See [the hypothesis](HYPOTHESIS.md), [evidence status](EVIDENCE.md) and [rights boundaries](RIGHTS.md).

## Ways to participate

Open an [issue](https://github.com/sheilastudios/genesis/issues/new) for discussion, or a [pull request](https://github.com/sheilastudios/genesis/pulls) for a concrete documentation change. A short proposal is welcome; a completed experiment is not required.

- **Propose tasks:** describe recurring work, later opportunities for reuse, changing requirements, and what useful performance would look like without prescribing the machinery an agent should build.
- **Challenge controls and measures:** identify competing explanations, experimenter hints, unequal resources, or ways a measure could reward documentation without useful behavior.
- **Suggest prior work:** link the source and explain the relevant mechanism, comparison or limitation. Work that narrows our novelty claims is welcome.
- **Share independently executed observations:** include negative, mixed and unexpected outcomes, not only successful examples.

Unexpected arrangements matter. Consultation, a small note, restricting or retiring an artifact, and choosing not to build anything may all be appropriate. Record who supplied advice and who initiated consultation; choosing to ask for help is not the same as requiring approval for every step.

## Reporting an observation

Identify what you actually observed, what you infer, and what remains unknown. Where available, include:

- Model/version, execution dates, environment, available tools, supplied instructions and examples, and exact re-entry cues.
- Task sequence, feedback rules, resource limits, operator assistance and consultation.
- The artifact's versions, evidence of later use, and subsequent task outcomes.
- Comparison conditions, construction and maintenance costs, failures, abandoned attempts and missing records.
- Reproduction instructions and shareable logs or artifacts, with a stable revision or commit identifier.

Separate **artifact**, **use** and **benefit**. Artifact creation alone does not establish improved performance. Episodes within one continuing history are dependent observations; identify separate fresh trajectories rather than counting every episode as an independent replication. Distinguish participant attempts, host-blocked actions and infrastructure faults.

If no published protocol was followed, label the contribution exploratory and describe your own conditions. Do not imply reproduction of an unavailable private seed. Incomplete reports can still be useful when their limitations are explicit.

## Evidence and protocol changes

Community-submitted observations are not automatically independently reviewed or reproduced Genesis results. Any synthesis should identify the submitter, source revision, review performed and reproduction status. Documentary review is not experimental reproduction.

Suggestions can inform future protocol versions. They do not silently modify a frozen experiment. Changes to frozen conditions must be separately versioned and identified as amendments or variants, with their timing and rationale preserved; retain original outcomes and report deviations.

## Share only material you can publish

Use synthetic or appropriately shareable examples. Do not submit credentials, private conversations, confidential source material or material you lack permission to disclose. This invitation does not release the private papers or operational governing materials, or change [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md). For proposed code or dataset contributions, state provenance and applicable permissions so reuse terms can be resolved before incorporation.
